THE TRANSFORMATION STORY

From Barren Farmland to a Thriving Green Ecosystem

Located in Pavagada, Brindavana representsVanaTvam’s flagship proof of concept. What began as 30 acres of largely depleted, dry agricultural land has been systematically transformed into a dense, multi-tiered native forest ecosystem.

By introducing rainwater swales, deep organic composting, and planting native drought-resistant tree species alongside Nakshatra Vana sacred groves, Brindavana now attracts over 40 species of birds, butterflies, and natural pollinators.

“Brindavana proved that when human effort sets the right foundation, nature takes over with incredible velocity.”

—VanaTvam Reforestation Team
