NATURE-FIRST LANDSCAPE

Co-existing with India's Premier Wildlife Corridor

Located near the pristine backwaters of Kabini, Anantavana is a 35-acre nature-first community designed to serve as a supportive buffer zone for the surrounding Bandipur and Nagarhole tiger reserves.

By planting native canopy species, protecting natural drainage paths, and avoiding invasive fences or loud commercial structures, Anantavana allows humans to live peacefully beside wildlife while actively expanding habitat boundaries.
