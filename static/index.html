FOREST IS YOU.
Not Just Land. A Way of Being.

AtVanaTvam, we believe the boundary between people and nature was never meant to exist. Our communities are designed to bring people closer to living ecosystems — not away from them. You don't just visit nature. You become a part of it.

Through managed farmland in Karnataka and sustainable farm communities near Bangalore, VanaTvam enables conscious individuals to invest in eco-friendly farmland that nurtures biodiversity while creating a timeless asset for future generations.

HUMAN PRESENCE AS ECOLOGICAL PROTECTION

CONSERVATION THAT INVITES PEOPLE IN

Traditional conservation often begins by keeping people away from nature.VanaTvam takes a different approach. We bring people into the ecosystem as owners, residents, and custodians — creating a model where human presence directly supports the growth and regeneration of nature. Because when people experience a living ecosystem, they develop a reason to protect it.

BIODIVERSITY BY DESIGN

We Don't Landscape.
We Create Ecosystems.

EveryVanaTvam community begins with the land itself. We study its soil, water, terrain, existing vegetation, and ecological potential to design landscapes where native forests, water bodies, habitat corridors, walking trails, and community spaces work together.

01

03

04

Self-Sustaining Forest

The objective is simple: Create an ecosystem that can eventually sustain, expand, and regenerate itself for generations.

WHEN NATURE TAKES OVER

We plant the beginning.
Nature builds the rest.

Vanatvam creates diverse and dense starter ecosystems. Over time, birds, bees, insects, and other natural agents begin carrying seeds, pollinating plants, and expanding vegetation beyond the areas originally planted. This is what we call an Evolving Ecosystem.

Human Effort Creates The Foundation

We carefully select 200+ native tree species, establish soil microbial networks, and construct rainwater harvesting swales.

Nature Creates The Future

Natural agents propagate seeds into dense multi-tier canopy forests — a process measured not in months, but in years and generations.

200+ VARIETIES OF TREES, PLANTS & HABITATS

Functioning Habitats Supporting Life

Across our communities,VanaTvam has introduced and conserved 200+ varieties of trees and plants, including rare and endangered native species. These aren't ornamental plantations — they are designed as functioning habitats that support an expanding network of life.

Indian Grey Hornbill

Feeds on native fig species (Ficus) planted across our multi-layered forest corridors.

Woolly-necked Stork

Thrives around wetlands, seasonal swales, and water-forest ponds in our Karnataka farm estates.

Asian Green Bee-eater

Restores natural insect control while nesting along pristine dirt mounds and flower pathways.

Baya Weaver

Weaves intricate nests on palm branches in our self-sustaining ecological farm plots near Bangalore.

Purple Heron

Frequents the quiet water bodies and riverfront sanctuaries of our Eeshavana community on the Cauvery.
