RIVERFRONT ECOSYSTEM

Where Sacred Water Meets Native Reforestation

Set along the serene riverbanks of Kollegala, Eeshavana offers rare private riverfront land parcels integrated with native riparian forests. The community is built around quiet water promenade trails, floating decks, and organic orchards.

Here, land owners can step directly onto riverfront trails, observe aquatic birdlife including Purple Herons and Storks, and experience the calming flow of the Cauvery River every day.
